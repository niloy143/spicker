*Supercharge* Your Time Tracking with Spicker!

Transform your TopTracker experience with powerful analytics and insights that help you optimize your productivity and work-life balance.

### Installation Guide

Getting started with Spicker is quick and easy! Follow these simple steps to install the extension from the Chrome Web Store:

1. **Open Chrome Web Store**: Visit the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/) in your Chrome browser.
2. **Search for Spicker**: Use the search bar to look for "Spicker" or click [here](https://chromewebstore.google.com/search/spicker) for direct results.
3. **Select the Extension**: Click on the Spicker extension from the search results to open its page.
4. **Add to Chrome**: Click the **"Add to Chrome"** button in the top-right corner of the page.
5. **Confirm Installation**: A popup will appear—click **"Add Extension"** to confirm and install.
6. **Access Spicker**: Once installed, you'll see the Spicker icon in your browser's extension toolbar. Click it anytime to open the popup dashboard or access full reports.

That's it! Spicker is now ready to supercharge your TopTracker experience. If you encounter any issues, ensure your Chrome browser is up to date.

### Key Features:
- Real-time Dashboard: Monitor daily and monthly progress at a glance
- Custom Hour Requirements: Set personalized weekly schedules (Mon-Fri 8hrs, Sat 4hrs, Sun off)
- Off-Day Management: Mark vacation days, holidays, and personal time off
- Advanced Reporting: Detailed tables with worked vs required hours analysis
- Visual Progress Bars: See your productivity trends with beautiful charts
- Date Range Analysis: Custom date picker for flexible reporting periods
- Monthly Navigation: Easy month-by-month time tracking review
